# 20-DAY-ROLLING-VOLATILITY
A 20 day volatility measures an asset's short-term price fluctuations over a trailing one-month period. This acts as a real-time gauge of any market stress or calm.

## What is the significance in using a 20-day-window and why i used it:
- A 20-day-window represents a one trading month this helps balance the responsiveness with statistical relevance of the data
- Spikes very quicky during market sell-offs, liquidity crunches, or macroeconomic shock
- Daily forward rolls reveals whether market stability is breaking down or returning

## Specific Practical uses in Finance
- Position sizing: One can adjust their trading leverage or their capital association when short-term risk expands or contracts
- Option Pricing: Informs near-term derivative pricing and historical comparison against implied volatility
- Specific Regime Identification: Classify specific market phases into distinct periods "tranquil" or "volatile" e.g. 2008 financial crisis
